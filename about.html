About Burger House

Good food, simple ingredients and a friendly place to enjoy a burger.

Our story

Burger House is an imaginary local burger shop created for our front-end group assignment. Our idea is simple: serve freshly prepared burgers using familiar ingredients and give customers an easy way to see the menu online.

The website keeps the same colors, typography, navigation and footer on every page so the whole project feels like one complete website.

What matters to us

Fresh ingredients

We focus on simple ingredients that look good and taste fresh.

Made to order

Our burgers are prepared when customers order instead of sitting around.

Friendly service

We want ordering food to feel easy, welcoming and straightforward.
