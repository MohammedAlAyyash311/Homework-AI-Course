About Burger House

Our Story

Burger House is an imaginary local burger shop created for our front-end group assignment. Our idea is simple: serve freshly prepared burgers using familiar ingredients.

The website keeps the same colors, typography, navigation and footer on every page.

What Matters to Us

Fresh Ingredients

We focus on simple ingredients that look good and taste fresh.

Made to Order

Our burgers are prepared when customers order.

Friendly Service

We want ordering food to feel easy and welcoming.
